About a year ago, I changed my epinephrine prescription from the AuviQ autoinjector to Neffy nasal spray. Neffy sounded interesting, as it wasn't an injector and looked like it would be significantly smaller than the AuviQ.

However, when I received the prescription, I was surprised to find that it wasn't smaller or more convenient to carry than the AuviQ at all. The advertising for neffy has a lot of photos of the nasal spray device, but what they don't show much is that it comes in a big, and most importantly thick blister pack. 

![Neffy Blister Pack](https://www.neffy.com/static/e1d0f7b5e2be452ae3d95ddf0e8889b1/9d66c/neffy-packaging.webp)

It also doesn't come with a case, but (at the time) you could get a carrying case by surrendering your personal information to neffy (I think it comes with the prescription now). The carrying case is designed to carry 2 neffy blister packs and is too thick to comfortably fit in a pocket like an AuviQ did. 

Instead of using their carrying case, I designed and 3D printed a carrying case to hold the neffy devices themselves instead of the blister packs. The case holds two devices, enclosing the plunger and nasal tube but notably does *not* enclose the rest of the neffy case. This makes it substantially thinner than Neffy's carrying case and also thinner than the blister packs. 

## Printing and Assembly

The model prints in 2 identical halves, held together by clips. I would recommend using a fair amount of infill, ~50% or more to make sure the clips do not break off. 
The hole that holds the nasal tube is a fairly tight fit, it may need some sanding or filing for the neffy device to fit and the case to close. The hole that holds the neffy plunger is a much looser fit, there should be no resistance when putting the plunger side of the neffy device in. If you encounter resistance when inserting the neffy plunger into the case, stop immediately to prevent the neffy from discharging.

A parametric .step file is also included if modifications need to be made to the fit for your printer or your neffy devices.
