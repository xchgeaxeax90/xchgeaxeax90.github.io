## Making the candy

The recipe I used is [Duff Goldman's Gummy Shark recipe](https://www.duff.com/recipes/shark-gummies).
For the green gummies, I made a half batch of the recipe with 1/4 teaspoon mint extract for flavoring.

For the red and green gummies, I made a 3/4 batch and divided it in
half for the two flavors. Red used a few drops of lorann raspberry
flavor and 3 packets of lime concentrate powder, blue used a few drops
of lorann blueberry flavor and 1 packet of lime concentrate powder.

Just before pouring the candy mixture into the molds, I whipped it
with a hand mixer to work enough air into the candy to make it
opaque. This worked reasonably well, though it's difficult to strike a
balance between enough foam to get the right color and making the
mixture too difficult to scoop and pour.

The mixture was poured into molds with a squeeze bottle.

Most of the dinosaur candies were remelted afterwards and recast into the mascot molds.
